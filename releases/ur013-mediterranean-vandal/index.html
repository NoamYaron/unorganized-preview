Mediterranean Vandal

shemesh

UR013 · 6 Mar 2026 · EP

Mediterranean vandal is here🏛️🌊. This album is a journey of contrasts: half the tracks are heavenly and calm, half are pure chaos. shemesh plays with heavy, distorted basslines against beautiful melodies on a choppy and groovy amen break samples In In shemesh’s own words: “Fast music is the love of my life. Breaking drums is my passion” A true love letter to fast music. don’t sleep on this one. Mix - Tzook Algazi (Zooki) Mastering - Ohad Nissim

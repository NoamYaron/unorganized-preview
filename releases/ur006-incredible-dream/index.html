Incredible Dream

Di Vundad

UR006 · 5 Sep 2024 · Album

Di Vundad (aka Haangli, The English Patient) was an improv 6 piece band that played in Tel Aviv from 2011-2013. The songs presented in this album are cuts from their “open rehearsal” format, and were recorded using one microphone. This material is being released about twelve years after its conception and with no particular regard to how it sounds. Noam Yaron (Mo Rayon) - Vocals Tamir Hasan - Bass Adiel Goldman - Drums Gil Abramov - Guitar Danny Odes - Guitar Yovav Arzi - Synth

XOE MY TRU LOVE

CHEAP LOVE HOTEL

UR011 · 15 Jan 2026 · Album

Catalog / DistroKid / Apple title is TRU. Bandcamp lists it as XOE MY TRUE LOVE.

When a man falls, the world crumbles, but he doesn't hit the ground. He lifts off. His heart is racing, stomach craving, mind numb. In the surrender, everything becomes possible. If Xoe is love, and love is life, then life is moving at full velocity. It is the promise of the infinite, staring back at the fallen. Until it dissolves, there is time to dream. The dream is mine. She is mine. Xoe, my true love.

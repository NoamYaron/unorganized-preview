XOE NO MORE

CHEAP LOVE HOTEL

UR012 · 19 Feb 2026 · Album

When a man falls, the world crumbles, and he hits the ground with a deafening thud. He doesn't lift off; he is pinned. His heart is lead, stomach churning, mind a frantic cage. In the wreckage, everything becomes impossible. If XOE was love, and love was a lie, then life is a standstill at zero velocity. It is the void of the infinite, mocking the fallen with a hollow stare. Now that it has dissolved, there is no more time to dream. The dream was a theft. She was never mine and she is no more. XOE no more.

Fantasy

Mo Rayon

UR009 · 28 Feb 2025 · Album

🎀 Another day, another fantasy. 🎀 "FANTASY" is a journey through groggy moments & shifting dreams. MO RAYON’s hypnotic, sedated vocals float over peculiar productions and drifting melodies, pulling you into the stillness of the night. "Singing songs about forgetting who you were - getting lost inside a big temple when the sun hits the corner of your eye. No need for introductions, the scene is set. The magic of the moon bubbles like a thunder..." In a major twist of fate, it seems that today, February 28th, is the planetary alignment of seven stars in our solar system. We suggest listening to the album while looking at the stars and fantasizing.

Available on Bandcamp only.

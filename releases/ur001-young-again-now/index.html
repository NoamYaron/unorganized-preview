Young Again Now

Mo Rayon

UR001 · 2 Oct 2023 · Album

“Young Again Now” is a collection of short moments inspired by MO RAYON’s hazy time in Japan. MO’s “Mantra” like vocals are accompanied by distant drums, distorted bass and smokey guitar lines that take you on a journey of mixed feelings, loneliness, lost love and forgotten hopes. "Love is terror and I'm scared tonight..."

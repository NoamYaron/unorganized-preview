For press, guest mixes, and booking, write to contact@chopshopunorganized.com. Tell us what you need — a record, a person, or a sentence we can stand behind. High-res artwork and a one-line fact sheet live with the catalog.

UNORGANIZED REKORDS is a Tel Aviv label under Chop Shop. The catalog runs from 2023 to 2026 and currently holds 14 releases — jungle, haze, leftover pop, and a few things that only exist on Bandcamp because the internet changed its mind.

UNORGANIZED REKORDS is a Tel Aviv record label and part of Chop Shop (opens in a new tab), the clothing and ideas shop that’s been “taking advantage of fabrics, abusing trends, discriminating cuts and maintaining low standards since 2000 and something.”

The label puts out work by Mo Rayon and a small, shifting roster — jungle, hip-hop, leftover pop, improvised wreckage, and records that only exist on Bandcamp because the internet changed its mind.

A real leftover isn’t intentional. A true one just happens, and we put a catalog number on it.
